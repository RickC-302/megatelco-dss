Weighted Score Breakdown

Each stacked segment shows a criterion’s weighted contribution to the strategy’s total score (0–100).

Scoring notes: a modeled campaign’s financial score is its positive active profit divided by the strongest positive active profit (capped at 100); negative profit scores 0. Strategy 3 receives a neutral 50 because its profit is not quantified in the report. Churn scores use observed segment churn rates (0–6m tenure 52.94%, month-to-month 42.71%, electronic check 45.29%) relative to 52.94%; Strategy 1 uses the equal-weight mean of its two targeted segments, Strategy 2 has no direct churn-mitigation target in the report, and Strategy 3 uses the electronic-check rate. These are exposure proxies, not estimated causal churn reductions. Safety score = 100 × actual input / (actual input + break-even threshold); Strategy 3 is neutral 50 because no break-even model is available. Feasibility anchors (90, 75, 55) reflect the report’s short-, short-to-medium-, and long-term implementation sequence; this component averages the anchor with a lower-cost-is-better min-max comparison of modeled total campaign costs. Strategy 3’s unmodeled cost component is neutral 50. Treat these transparent scoring assumptions as decision aids, not measured outcomes.

Model definitions and assumptions

  • Retention: accepted customers = N_target × r_acc; retained customers = accepted customers × r_ret; incremental revenue = retained customers × M × T; campaign cost = accepted customers × C_camp; profit = revenue minus campaign cost.
  • Streaming: upgraded customers = N_eligible × r_up; incremental revenue = upgraded customers × Delta_R × T_eval; total cost = upgraded customers × (Delta_S × T_eval + C_promo); profit = revenue minus total cost.
  • ROI is incremental profit divided by total campaign cost. If cost is zero, ROI is N/A. Results are deterministic expected-value estimates, so fractional customer counts are not rounded in the calculations.
  • Retention break-even incremental retention probability = C_camp / (M × T). Streaming break-even additional monthly revenue = Delta_S + C_promo / T_eval.
  • All monetary values are in US dollars. Retention costs are per accepted customer. Streaming costs include additional service costs over the evaluation horizon and the one-time promotion. This version does not model uncertainty, discounting, customer-level records, or cash-flow timing.
